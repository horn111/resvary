THESIS

Operator Console is a settlement instrument, not a generic analytics dashboard. It gives an operator enough context to explain any balance change and only the recovery actions that are safe by construction.

OWN-WORLD

The console inherits Resvary's near-black continuous field, off-white hierarchy, square controls, thin construction rules, Archivo for propositions, and JetBrains Mono for identifiers, states, and measurements. No gradients, glass, decorative grids, or rounded SaaS cards.

STORY

The operator arrives at the current system situation, scans exceptions, follows a customer or entity through one chronological ledger, opens a receipt dossier that links charge to receipt, reservation, price, and entries, then performs a narrowly constrained recovery with a mandatory reason and durable action record.

FIRST VIEWPORT

A compact left rail identifies the project and four destinations. The main viewport opens with “Every balance has a reason.”, a terse operational situation strip, a dominant chronological activity ledger, and a right-side context pane that explains the selected record. Overdue reservations and dead letters are visible without turning the page into a wall of metric cards.

FORM

Desktop-first operator workspace with dense evidence, keyset-paginated lists, legible tabular numbers, robust keyboard focus, and a deliberate stacked mobile layout. The action panel uses explicit preview, confirmation copy, disabled/loading/error states, and no modal unless protected focus is required.

SEED

Command Ledger — the assigned surface concept from seed 2b1c1c43.

FINISH

Industrial, restrained, and inspectable. The craft should feel closer to a clearing terminal than an admin template: flat surfaces, exact language, visible provenance, one authored ledger-arrival moment, and no ornamental data visualization.
